What it does

A break you can't scroll past.

Most timers ask politely and get ignored. Nebbia covers every display when time is up, and is careful about when it does.

The whole screen rests

Every display blurs behind a Liquid Glass countdown. Your windows are untouched underneath.

A heads-up first

Thirty seconds before, a small pill counts down so you can finish the sentence. Tap +5 min if you need it.

Never mid-call

A break waits while your camera or microphone is in use, or while a video or presentation is playing.

Steps away with you

The timer pauses when you leave or lock your Mac. Away long enough, and that counts as the break.

Hold yourself to it

Limit postpones, switch on strict mode to remove Skip, and keep a streak of breaks actually taken.

Private by design

No account, no tracking and no internet connection. Your stats stay on your Mac.

First launch

One extra step the first time.

Nebbia is made by an independent developer and isn't registered with Apple's paid developer programme, so macOS asks you to approve it once.

  1. Drag Nebbia into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. macOS says it can't verify the app. Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  4. That's it. Look for the two overlapping circles in your menu bar.
Questions

Good to know.

Can I get out of a break if I really need to?

Yes. Skip Break, or the Esc key, ends it straight away. If you'd rather not have the option, strict mode removes it.

Will it blur my screen during a meeting?

It's built not to. Nebbia holds the break while your camera or microphone is in use, or while an app such as a video player or a presentation is keeping the display awake, and starts it once you're free. A silent screen share with none of those can't be detected.

What permissions does it need?

None to run. It doesn't ask for Accessibility or Screen Recording access and never connects to the internet. Calendar access is requested only if you switch on "Keep breaks clear of meetings".

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac on macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. The glass effects need that version of macOS.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

Apps from developers outside Apple's paid programme show that warning. You approve Nebbia once in System Settings › Privacy & Security, and the steps come with the download.
